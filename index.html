01 — About

What I do

I am a Data Engineer with 4 years of experience in designing and maintaining data solutions for the FMCG and mining industries.

My work focuses on building reliable ETL pipelines, developing data warehouse solutions, and delivering high-quality datasets for analytics and reporting. I have experience working with Microsoft Fabric, SQL Server, AWS, Python, and workflow orchestration tools such as Airflow. I also enjoy turning complex data into meaningful insights through Power BI, Tableau, and Grafana while working closely with business users to deliver practical data solutions.
